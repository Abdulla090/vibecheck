# Royal VIP Clinic & Aesthetics Gateway (Erbil & Sulaymaniyah)

High-performance, bilingual (Sorani Kurdish RTL & English LTR) digital gateway for luxury cosmetic dentistry and dermatological clinics in the Kurdistan Region of Iraq (Gulan St, Erbil & Salim St, Sulaymaniyah).

Live Production Demo: [https://vibecheck-gamma-nine.vercel.app](https://vibecheck-gamma-nine.vercel.app)

---

## Key Features

1. **Medical Luxury Obsidian Aesthetic (Anti-AI Slop & Apple HIG Standards)**:
   - Obsidian palette (`#0B0F17` backdrop, `#121826` hairline cards), emerald surgical green (`#10B981`) and champagne accents (`#D4AF37`).
   - Clean typography using **Vazirmatn** (Kurdish Sorani) and **Outfit** (Latin). Strictly no Inter font, no generic AI gradients.

2. **Instant Bilingual Switcher (Kurdish Sorani RTL & English LTR)**:
   - Full idiomatic Sorani Kurdish translations and Apple-grade English.
   - Dynamic document `dir="rtl"` / `dir="ltr"` and `lang` switching.

3. **Interactive Before & After Image Comparison Slider**:
   - Touch and mouse draggable split-screen comparison slider.
   - Live clinical cases: Hollywood Smile (German E-max Veneers BL1), Clear Aligners Orthodontics, Laser Skin Resurfacing, and Swiss Straumann Dental Implants.

4. **VIP Procedure Catalog with Iraqi Dinar Pricing**:
   - Starting prices in USD ($) and dynamically computed Iraqi Dinar (د.ع IQD).
   - Procedure category filtering, warranty badges, and "Select & Book" trigger.

5. **Direct 1-Click WhatsApp VIP Booking Engine**:
   - Pre-populated structured booking message: procedure, patient name, phone number, time slot window, and notes.
   - Generates formatted `https://wa.me/...` URL ready to send to clinic reception in 1 tap.

6. **Local Iraqi Payment & Trust Badges**:
   - First Iraqi Bank (FIB) 0% installment option.
   - FastPay, ZainCash, and Cash at Reception desk.
   - German Ivoclar Vivadent and Swiss Straumann certifications.

7. **Agency Pitch Mode / Demo Settings Drawer**:
   - Discreet floating settings drawer allowing on-the-fly clinic rebranding (Name, WhatsApp number, City, Doctor name, Exchange rate).
   - Quick presets for Gulan St (Erbil), Salim St (Sulaymaniyah), and Empire World.
   - Included $700 close pitch strategy script for closing $350 upfront deposits in 15 minutes.

8. **VIP Clinic & Pharmacy Management CRM (`/crm`)**:
   - **Executive KPI Dashboard**: Live patient pipeline value, active consultations, today's chair queue count, low-stock alerts, and dual currency ($ USD & IQD) daily revenue tracking.
   - **Patient & Customer Pipeline (Lead Management)**: 5-stage Kanban flow (New Leads, Consultation Booked, In Treatment, Completed, Post-Op Follow-up) with 1-click stage advancement, search, VIP tier filtering, and new lead creation.
   - **Pharmacy & Surgical Inventory Tracker**: Comprehensive SKU control with stock level monitoring, minimum safety thresholds, batch numbers, automated expiry tracking (<90-day alert), quick in-line count adjustments, and interactive prescription dispensing.
   - **1-Click WhatsApp Supplier Restock PO**: Direct 1-tap WhatsApp purchase order dispatcher to medical suppliers (Straumann, Ivoclar, Allergan, GSK, Septodont) when stock falls below safety minimums.
   - **Daily Appointment Scheduler & Clinical Queue**: Real-time doctor & chair queue with room allocation, status tracking (Waiting, In Chair, Completed), and 1-tap WhatsApp "Your VIP Suite is Ready" alerts.
   - **Digital Prescriptions & Clinical Invoices**: Automated cryptographic security hashes, bilingual dosage/intake instructions, dual currency breakdown, print/PDF layout, and instant 1-tap WhatsApp dispatch to patients.
   - **Branch Switcher & Bilingual Toggle**: Real-time switching between Erbil (Gulan St), Sulaymaniyah (Salim St), and Erbil (Empire World) with Sorani Kurdish RTL and English LTR mirroring.
